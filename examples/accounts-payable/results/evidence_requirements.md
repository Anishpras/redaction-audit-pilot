# Evidence requirements: AP agent executes a supplier payment

Our accounts-payable agent may execute a supplier payment only if a manager approval exists for the same invoice, the approver manages the same cost centre the payment is booked to, the amount and currency match the approval (amount at most the approved amount), the approval was granted before the payment and had not expired, the approval was not withdrawn at or before the payment, the approver holds a manager role, the approver is not the person who requested the payment (four-eyes, expressed natively with relation 'different'), the payee bank account was checked against the vendor master and is allowlisted, and the approval references an invoice document.

A redacted log can be audited against this policy only if it keeps the evidence below. Each condition is listed with its *need type*; the guidance says which redaction forms preserve it. Generated by redaction_audit 1.2.0.

## same_invoice (equality)

- **Rule:** `approval.payload.invoice_id` equals `payment.payload.invoice_id`
- **What the log must keep:** Keep both values, or replace them with pseudonyms that stay consistent across every event (this also holds for 'different' rules such as four-eyes checks) the condition compares (scope run or global). Uniform masks remove the relation. Per-event pseudonyms remove it too, and if undeclared they turn every authorised action into a false alarm.
- **Tested views that keep it:** full, alias_per_run, prod_current, prod_proposed, amounts_banded, prod_proposed_amounts_ranked, iban_last4_declared, ap_redactor
- **Tested views that lose it:** vendor_default_mask (unverifiable)
